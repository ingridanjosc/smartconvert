Upscale and enhance an image

Increase pixel dimensions by 2× or 4× and control sharpness, brightness and contrast in your browser.

How to increase image resolution

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Select original, 2× or 4× resolution.
  3. Adjust sharpness, brightness and contrast.
  4. Download as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Honest image enhancement

This free tool uses high-quality browser scaling and visual adjustments. It increases dimensions and clarity, but it does not claim to reconstruct missing detail with artificial intelligence.

What increasing resolution means

Upscaling creates more pixels for larger display dimensions. Sharpening improves visible edges, while brightness and contrast adjust appearance. These controls cannot reveal details that were never captured.

Choose 2× or 4× carefully

Use 2× for faster, moderate enlargement and 4× for larger output. Too much sharpening may create noise or outlines. This tool helps presentation graphics, small photos and web assets, but it cannot reliably repair severe blur or unreadable text. Try 2× if a large image exceeds browser memory.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool use AI?

No. The current free tool uses browser-based scaling and sharpening.

Why can 4× fail on a large photo?

Very large images may exceed the memory available in your browser. Use 2× in that situation.
