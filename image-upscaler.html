  1. Choose an image.

Honest image enhancement

This free tool uses high-quality browser scaling and visual adjustments. It increases dimensions and clarity, but it does not claim to reconstruct missing detail with artificial intelligence.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool use AI?

No. The current free tool uses browser-based scaling and sharpening.

Why can 4× fail on a large photo?

Very large images may exceed the memory available in your browser. Use 2× in that situation.
