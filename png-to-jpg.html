Smaller files for easy sharing

JPG is often smaller than PNG for photographs. Transparent areas are placed on a white background during conversion.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparency?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent pixels become white.

Can I choose the image quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider before converting.
