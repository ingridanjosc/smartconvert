FREE PDF TOOL

Split PDF and extract pages

Choose individual pages or a page range and create a new PDF in seconds.

How to extract PDF pages

  1. Choose the PDF from your device.
  2. Enter pages such as 1,3,5-8.
  3. Click “Extract and download”.
  4. Save the new PDF containing only those pages.

Extract only what you need

Removing unnecessary pages makes documents easier to share, review and organize. The original file is not changed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I select non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Use commas, for example 1,3,7.

Can I extract a page range?

Yes. Enter a range such as 4-12.
