Change a JPG or JPEG image into a high-quality PNG file directly in your browser.

How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Choose a JPG or JPEG image.
  2. Check the dimensions.
  3. Click Convert to PNG.
  4. Download the finished PNG.

Private and watermark-free

The conversion runs on your device. SmartConvert does not upload or store your image, and no watermark is added.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting to PNG improve the original quality?

No converter can restore detail already lost in a JPG, but PNG prevents additional lossy compression.

Will the background become transparent?

No. Converting formats does not automatically remove the existing background.
