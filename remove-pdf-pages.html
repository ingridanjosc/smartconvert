Remove pages from a PDF

Delete unwanted PDF pages and download a new copy without uploading your document.

How to delete PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF document.
  2. Enter pages such as 2,4,7-9.
  3. Click Remove and download.
  4. Check the new PDF before deleting the original.

Your original PDF stays unchanged

SmartConvert creates a separate copy containing the pages you kept. Processing happens inside your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove several page ranges?

Yes. Separate pages and ranges with commas, such as 2,5-8,11.

Can I remove every page?

No. The finished PDF must contain at least one page.
