Rotate PDF pages online

Fix sideways or upside-down PDF pages and download a corrected copy.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Select 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
  3. Click Rotate and download.
  4. Open the new copy and verify the pages.

Private browser processing

The rotation runs locally on your device. Your original document remains unchanged.

Frequently asked questions

Does this rotate every page?

Yes. The current tool applies the chosen rotation to all pages.

Does rotating reduce PDF quality?

No. Page rotation metadata changes without rendering the pages as images.
